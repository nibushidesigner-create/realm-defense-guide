Hero Evaluation Method

How to decide which Realm Defense hero to raise first

Hero value changes by account stage. Early accounts need reliable wave control and anti-air coverage; mid-game accounts need Boss handling and lane insurance; late-game tournament players care more about blessed heroes, cooldown cycles, and tower support.

Early campaign priorities

Raise heroes that solve multiple common problems: flying enemies, fast ground leaks, and bulky waves. Fee and Lancelot cover basic progression, while control heroes such as Efrigid or Connie make difficult lanes easier to stabilize.

Mid-game role coverage

Once maps split into several lanes, one damage hero is not enough. Build a small roster with one crowd-control pick, one burst or Boss answer, and one hero who can delay enemies while towers finish the wave.

Tournament judgment

In tournaments, the blessed hero can be worth using even when the normal tier list would not favor them. Compare the blessed bonus with the map type: long choke maps reward control, while short open maps often demand faster burst.

Practical Upgrade Notes

Read hero value by the problem they solve

Realm Defense heroes are easiest to compare when each one is treated as a tool for a specific map problem. A hero with excellent damage can still be inefficient if the current stage needs delay, anti-air, or split-lane coverage. Before spending gems or elixir, identify the wave that ends the run and choose the hero role that directly changes that wave.

Control heroes

Freeze, fear, stun, teleport, and summon control are valuable because they extend tower time-in-range. These heroes are strongest on maps with bends, merges, and dangerous fast waves. If enemies are leaving the main tower zone alive, control usually beats another small damage upgrade.

Damage and Boss heroes

Burst heroes should be judged by whether their cooldown lines up with the Boss path or armored wave. Strong burst used outside the tower zone can feel impressive but waste the only timing window that matters. Save these heroes for stages where the main target can be held still long enough to convert damage into a clear.

Support and economy heroes

Support heroes become better when the map already has a strong tower location. Haste, buffs, summons, and delay effects multiply the best tower zone rather than replacing it. If a stage has poor tower coverage, pair support with a hero who can hold enemies in place first.

Raise now

  • The hero solves a repeated leak type such as flyers, runners, or Boss health.
  • A new rank changes the skill behavior, uptime, or survivability in a visible way.
  • The hero appears in several lineups you can actually field today.

Wait first

  • The hero is only useful for one rare map type and your core team is still weak.
  • Your current failure is tower placement or cooldown timing rather than roster depth.
  • The purchase would delay a rank checkpoint that improves a hero you already use.

Review later

  • New tournament weeks make a previously niche hero important as the blessed pick.
  • A world introduces enemies that punish your old team pattern.
  • Balance changes or new maps make old assumptions less reliable.
