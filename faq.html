Realm Defense Guide FAQ

This FAQ explains how to use the guide database and how the recommendations are written. It is designed for players who want a practical route through campaign stages, hero upgrades, tower decisions, and weekly tournament attempts without treating any single list as a permanent rule.

What makes this site different from a basic wiki page?

A wiki table can tell you where a hero unlocks or which world a map belongs to. This guide adds the player decision layer: when a hero actually solves a map, why a tower location is worth upgrading, what to review after a failed run, and how to adjust a lineup when the recommended hero is missing.

Which hero should a new player invest in first?

Start with heroes that solve repeated campaign failures. If enemies leave your tower range too quickly, invest in control. If flyers are leaking, invest in anti-air coverage or ranged support. If Boss stages are the wall, save resources for burst timing and lane stability. A smaller roster with useful ranks is usually stronger than many unlocked heroes with no clear job.

How should I read a stage guide?

First look at the map image and identify the main route, flyer path, and exit risk. Then read the threat label and guide notes. The goal is not to copy a fixed build order blindly; the goal is to understand why a stage fails and which single change is most likely to solve it.

Why do some recommendations focus on tower timing instead of hero tiers?

Realm Defense is a tower defense game before it is a hero collection game. Even strong heroes lose value when towers are placed in short-range positions or upgraded before the dangerous wave appears. Good tower timing turns hero control into real damage because enemies stay inside the kill zone longer.

How should tournament advice be used?

Tournament maps change weekly, so advice is written as a review process. Scout the map, decide the blessed hero's job, record the failure wave, and refine one timing issue per run. A blessed hero can be central, limited to support, or occasionally carried by two stronger partners depending on map shape.

How often is the site updated?

The guide is updated when pages need clearer explanations, when missing stage or blog content is added, and when layout or navigation affects readability. Balance can change, so recommendations are treated as practical planning notes rather than permanent rankings.

Can I report an outdated recommendation?

Yes. Use the Contact page and include the page URL, hero or stage name, and the reason the advice should be reviewed. Useful reports explain the actual failure pattern or updated behavior, not only that a hero feels stronger or weaker.

Realm Defense 攻略站 FAQ

这个 FAQ 说明本站攻略应该怎么读。本站不是只给一个固定排行，而是帮助玩家理解主线关卡、英雄培养、防御塔升级和锦标赛尝试背后的判断逻辑。

本站和普通 wiki 页面有什么区别？

Wiki 表格通常能告诉你英雄在哪里解锁、地图属于哪个世界。本站会进一步补上玩家决策：某个英雄什么时候真正解决地图问题，为什么某个塔位值得升级，失败后该复盘什么，以及缺少推荐英雄时该怎么按功能替换。

新手应该先培养哪个英雄？

先培养能解决反复失败原因的英雄。敌人太快走出塔区，就补控场；飞行怪漏得多，就补防空或远程支援；Boss 关卡卡住，就规划爆发窗口和普通路线稳定性。少量英雄养到可用觉醒，通常比解锁很多但都不成型更稳定。

关卡攻略应该怎么读？

先看地图图片，确认主路线、飞行路线和出口风险；再看威胁标签和攻略说明。重点不是机械复制固定升塔顺序，而是理解这一关为什么会失败，以及下一次最应该调整哪一个点。

为什么很多建议强调升塔时机，而不是只看英雄强度？

Realm Defense 本质上仍然是塔防游戏。再强的英雄，如果塔位覆盖很短、或金币太早花在错误位置，也会发挥不出价值。合理的升塔时机能把英雄控场转化成实际伤害，因为敌人会在主火力区停留更久。

锦标赛攻略应该怎么用？

锦标赛地图每周变化，所以本站更强调复盘流程：先侦察地图，判断祝福英雄的实际岗位，记录失败波次，再逐次修正技能和升塔节奏。祝福英雄可能是核心，也可能只是辅助岗位，取决于地图形状和敌人压力。

本站多久更新一次？

当攻略说明需要更清晰、缺少关卡或文章内容、页面导航影响阅读时，本站会继续更新。游戏平衡可能变化，所以本站推荐更适合作为实用规划，而不是永久固定排行。

可以反馈过时内容吗？

可以。请通过 Contact 页面 提交页面链接、英雄或关卡名称，以及建议修改的原因。有效反馈最好说明实际失败点或机制变化，而不只是说某个英雄强或弱。
