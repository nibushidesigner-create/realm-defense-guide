About Realm Defense Guide Database

Realm Defense Guide Database is an unofficial fan-made strategy site for Realm Defense players. It organizes practical notes for hero abilities, campaign stages, tower mechanics, lineup planning, and tournament runs.

The goal is to make guide content easier to scan on desktop and mobile: players can quickly compare heroes, review stage maps, check tower plans, and prepare weekly tournament attempts.

Editorial approach

The guide focuses on practical decision-making: where to place heroes, when to use skills, which tower zone to upgrade, and how to adapt a lineup when a map changes. It is not an official game database.

关于 Realm Defense 攻略资料库

Realm Defense Guide Database 是非官方玩家攻略站，整理 Realm Defense 的英雄能力、战役关卡、地图、防御塔机制、阵容搭配和锦标赛思路。

本站目标是让攻略内容在网页和移动端都更容易阅读：玩家可以快速比较英雄、查看关卡地图、复盘塔位计划，并准备每周锦标赛冲分。

内容原则

攻略重点是可执行的判断：英雄站位、技能释放时机、主火力区升塔顺序，以及地图变化时如何调整阵容。本站不是官方游戏资料库。
